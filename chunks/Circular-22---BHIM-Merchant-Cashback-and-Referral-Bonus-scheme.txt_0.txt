Dear Sir / Madam, 
Subject: Launch of Referral and Merchant Cashback scheme for BHIM users. 
The Government of India (GOI) has launched two schemes to promote the usage of digital payments 
through BHIM and *99#. The schemes launched are –  
(a) Referral Bonus Scheme for individuals  
(b) Cashback Scheme for merchants  
 
The scheme was launched on 14th April, 2017 by Hon’ble Prime Minister of India, to popularize usage of 
digital payment among masses. Excerpts of the scheme is given below –  
A) Referral Bonus Scheme for Individuals: 
The scheme is to incentivize an existing user of BHIM to bring new users on the BHIM platform. The bonus 
will be paid to both the referrer and the new user of BHIM (referee) and the referral will be considered
