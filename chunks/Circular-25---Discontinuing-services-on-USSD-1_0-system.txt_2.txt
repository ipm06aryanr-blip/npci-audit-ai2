In USSD 1.0 (existing *99#), 51 banks were live. Post the introduction of USSD 2.0, these banks have 
started migrating to new system, after performing necessary migration process (Documentation & 
certification). 41 banks have already moved to USSD 2.0 and remaining banks are in the process of 
doing so. All the new members are on-boarded on USSD 2.0 only. NPCI has made both the systems 
active (USSD 1.0 & 2.0) to facilitate this migration up to 31st July 2017.  
USSD 1.0 system shall discontinue from 1st August 2017. Members are advised to take note of it and 
circulate to their respective internal teams for timely migration to USSD 2.0 if not done so far.
