SUB: Scanning UPI credentials integrated in Bharat QR through UPI PSP Application 
 
This bears reference to the NPCI circular: NPCI /2017-18/PD/004 dated on August 10th, 2017 
about “Integration of Bharat QR with UPI credential” and emphasizes on compliance with 
Merchant QR code specification V4.0 and ensuring that all Bharat QR deployed post 15th Sept, 
2017 should contain UPI credentials. 
 
With the introduction of above, All UPI PSP applications should be able to scan & pay using 
both UPI QR as well as Bharat QR (integrated with UPI credentials). Further in this regards, 
NPCI had sent communication to all the UPI PSPs and had released the technical specification
