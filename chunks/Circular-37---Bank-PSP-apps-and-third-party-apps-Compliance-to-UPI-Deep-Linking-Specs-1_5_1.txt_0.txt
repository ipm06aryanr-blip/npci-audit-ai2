NPCI/ UPI/ OC No. 37/ 2017-18 
 
 
 
 
            1st November 2017 
 
To, 
All Member Banks - Unified Payments Interface (UPI) 
 
Dear Sir/Madam, 
Subject: Bank PSP apps and third party apps Compliance to UPI Deep linking Specs 1.5.1 
 
This is with reference to compliance to UPI OC 18 “Bank compliances to enable UPI Merchant 
ecosystem”, issued on 20th March 2017 and “UPI Deep linking Specs 1.5.1”. It is observed that many 
Bank PSP apps and third party apps live in the ecosystem today, are failing to comply with UPI Deep 
Linking Specs 1.5.1. As a result, PSP apps and third party apps scanning the QR or responding to 
intent are failing to pass the critical parameters populated by the merchants in the QR/ Intent 
strings. 
The challenges faced with such non-compliant apps is discouraging merchants to adopt UPI for 
payments. The Banks are therefore requested to ensure compliance to UPI Deep Linking Specs 1.5.1 
latest by 30th Nov’2017. 
Members are requested for compliance.
