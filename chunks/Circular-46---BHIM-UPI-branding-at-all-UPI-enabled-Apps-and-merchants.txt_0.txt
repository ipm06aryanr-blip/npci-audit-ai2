Subject: BHIM UPI branding at all UPI enabled Apps and merchants 
 
The UPI ecosystem comprises of multiple UPI applications which are owned by the 
banks and 3rd party UPI enabled Apps sponsored by member banks as PSPs. We draw 
your reference to our Circular No. NPCI /UPI/OC No. 21/2017-18 dated 8th June, 
2017. 
Further, member banks are advised as under: 
1. All UPI enabled apps (Banks and 3rd party) may change name to BHIM <App 
name> UPI App. They can display BHIM UPI on selecting the 
payment/transfer. 
 
2. Similarly, all the merchants accepting UPI payments (QR, Intent, Collect etc.) 
to display BHIM UPI branding. 
This will help consumer to recall all UPI enabled Apps and merchants with BHIM UPI
