2. Incentive disbursement process 
 
NPCI analytics team shall carry out the process to find out the eligible users/merchants. For 
BHIM new users it shall be done daily and for others it shall be done monthly. 
 
The final eligible user list in the appropriate file shall be pushed to banks through the NACH 
system. 
 
Incentive amount to be credited to eligible user’s bank a/c linked against the UPI ID directly.
