Annexure A – Scheme details  
1. About the scheme 
 
MeitY through its notification ( No-12/13/2017-DPD-MeitY) dated 28th March 2018 has extended & 
modified the existing BHIM referral bonus scheme for individuals and BHIM cashback scheme for 
merchants.   
The scheme incentives both for customers and merchants and can be broadly classified in following 4 
components:  
 
Incentive for on-boarding on BHIM APP (New Users) 
 
Incentive on transactions over BHIM UPI App(s) of the bank & BHIM App (All users) 
 
Incentive for BHIM merchants 
 
Incentive for BHIM UPI merchants 
Component 1- Incentive for onboarding on BHIM APP 
 
This incentive is only for new BHIM app users. 

