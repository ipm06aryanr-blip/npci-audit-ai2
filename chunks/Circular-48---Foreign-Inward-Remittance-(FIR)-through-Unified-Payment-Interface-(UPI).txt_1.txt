FIR through UPI is a credit push similar to extant NEFT and IMPS, the incremental advantage provided 
is credit to beneficiary can be done basis a UPI ID, in addition to account no. and IFSC. 
To facilitate FIR transactions in UPI we have incorporated new elements, viz. “institution block” and 
“initiation mode-12” as an identifier for FIR transaction. These tags in UPI contain relevant
