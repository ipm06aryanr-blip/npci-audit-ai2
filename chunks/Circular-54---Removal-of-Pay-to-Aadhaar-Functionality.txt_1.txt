AADHAAR number is a sensitive information and the revised framework about its usage in the payment 
landscape is still evolving. With this background, we proposed removal of “Pay to AADHAAR 
“functionality in both UPI & IMPS before the steering committee (meeting held on 5th July 2018). The 
proposal of removing the “AADHAAR number” functionality was approved by the steering committee.  
“Pay to AADHAAR “functionality shall be removed from both UPI & IMPS from 31st August 2018. All 
member banks should remove this functionality both as remitter and beneficiary. Also all interface 
currently offering this functionality, such as UPI apps and third party apps should remove this option 
from their respective apps by 31st August 2018. 
Member banks are requested to make note of the above and circulate the information to relevant 
stakeholders for necessary action.
