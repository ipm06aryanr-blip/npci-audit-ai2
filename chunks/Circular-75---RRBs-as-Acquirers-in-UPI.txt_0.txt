Subject: On-boarding RRBs as Acquirers in UPI 
Unified Payments Interface (UPI) has 141 members live in the ecosystem at present. Out of these 53 
members act as Acquirers.  
It had earlier been approved for the Regional Rural Banks (RRBs) to participate in UPI as Issuers. A 
total of 33 RRBs are live as Issuers in UPI at present. 
It is further advised that the Regional Rural Banks (RRBs) can also participate in UPI as Acquirers 
subject to RRB having a mobile banking permission from Reserve Bank of India and post fulfilling the 
necessary certification requirements of NPCI. 
Members banks are requested to take note of the same.
