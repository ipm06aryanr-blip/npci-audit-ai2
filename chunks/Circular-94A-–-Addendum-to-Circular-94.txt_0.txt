Addendum to OC 94 
In addition to the procedures and guidelines stated in NPCI/UPI/OC No. 94/2020-21 for allowing 
TPAP to route transactions through the secondary UPI ID. 
2. The TPAP and PSP have to: 
a. Ensure transparent routing of transactions through the secondary UPI ID. The TPAP shall use 
code ‘10’ in the ref Category tag while initiating the payment through the back up UPI ID.  
b. Ensure transaction routing through the secondary UPI ID is valid only till the time primary PSP 
is unavailable. As soon as the primary PSP is available, the user will be restored back to the 
primary UPI ID for all UPI transactions.
