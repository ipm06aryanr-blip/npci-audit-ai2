Dear Sir/Madam, 
Subject:  Change in cutover cycles in UPI – (w.e.f – 28th January 2019)  
 
NPCI implemented the process of four settlements for UPI effective December 2016. 
1) NPCI cuts over the transactions for the 4th settlement cycle during the weekdays at 
17:30 hours at present and funds settlement for this cutover are completed as per 
the settlement processing in RBI RTGS system on the same day.  In cases of public 
holidays and RTGS holidays, NPCI carries out fund settlement/s on the next working 
day. 
2) Volumes in UPI have seen significant growth in the recent past. We have also received 
inputs from members with regard to few delays observed in the settlement timelines
