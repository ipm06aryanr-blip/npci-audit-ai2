UPI QR
UPI QR is another payment mode in UPI app. QR code 
can be used to pay or receive money. Each user has 
their respective QR code under the User Profile section. 
They can share their QR code to receive money and to 
pay they need to select the QR Scan option on the 
home page and do the payment. It is easy and quick.
