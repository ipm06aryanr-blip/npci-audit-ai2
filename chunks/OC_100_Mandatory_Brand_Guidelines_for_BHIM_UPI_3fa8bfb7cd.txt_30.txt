About the App:
One of the leading 
mobile-to-mobile payment app. 
Make UPI payments, transfer 
money from your bank account.
Using this app you can do a lot of 
transaction at ease. You can use 
BHIM UPI payments mode, 
recharge your mobile / DTH, pay 
your bills and make instant 
payment at retail stores. All you 
need to do is link your bank 
account on this app with your 
registered mobile number and start 
the experience.
UPI ID is a unique ID that is used 
to make UPI payments in place of 
bank account details.
Transfer money using BHIM UPI:
You can send money, receive 
money from your contacts are from 
any one by sharing you UPI ID. 
You can transfer money instantly to 
any mobile number via BHIM UPI 
using UPI ID. You can check you 
account balance and add multiple 
bank accounts like ABC Bank, XZY 
Bank, 123 Bank.
