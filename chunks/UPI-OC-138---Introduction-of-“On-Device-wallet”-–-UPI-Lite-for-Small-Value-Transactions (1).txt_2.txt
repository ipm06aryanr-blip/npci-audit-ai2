3rd January 2022, NPCI has designed the “UPI Lite – On-Device wallet” (“UPI Lite”) functionality for UPI user 
(User). In phase 1, UPI Lite will process transactions in near offline mode i.e. debit offline and credit online, and 
at a later point, UPI Lite will process transactions in complete offline mode i.e. debit and credit both offline. 
A User of UPI App will have the option to enable UPI Lite i.e. “On-Device wallet” on her/his UPI App. Once 
enabled, the User can allocate funds from her/his bank account to UPI Lite. Such funds shall reside with the 
User’s bank in an escrow / pool / designated account and such balance shall reside on-device on the common 
library (CL) of the User’s UPI App.
