Annexure A 
A. Customer-facing Information and experience to be provided, in addition to specifics in the circular  
 
By UPI App, for UPI Lite enablement: 
1. The User’s (only) bank account will be debited for loading UPI Lite 
2. The User can disable UPI Lite services at any point of time from the UPI App and on disabling UPI Lite, 
balance funds will be credited back to the User’s account in real-time
