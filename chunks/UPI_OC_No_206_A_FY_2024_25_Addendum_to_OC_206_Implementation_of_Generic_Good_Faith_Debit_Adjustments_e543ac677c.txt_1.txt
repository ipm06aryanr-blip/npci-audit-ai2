BGGD – BENEFICIARY BANK RAISING GENERIC GOOD FAITH DEBIT ADJUSTMENT: 
Sponsor/Acquiring Bank can raise generic good-faith debit adjustments with mandate creation entry 
i.e. TRANID, RRN and other details (refer below validation of adjustments section point no.1 for details) 
for IPO/Mandate with block functionality executions ONLY on remitter/issuing bank. TAT for raising a 
BGGD adjustment is maximum of 45 Days from the mandate creation date, sponsor bank should raise 
execution as per the actual TAT set for each individual mandate entities from the mandate end date. If 
the Sponsor bank raise BGGD after the set TAT, then issuing bank can reject the same with reason 
code RGD1. If the BGGD adjustment is raised after 45 days, then URCS will decline the same. Once
