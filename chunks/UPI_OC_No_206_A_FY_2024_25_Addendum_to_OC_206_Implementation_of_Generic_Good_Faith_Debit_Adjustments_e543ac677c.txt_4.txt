No penalty is applicable for Generic Good Faith Debit Adjustment, sponsor/acquiring banks 
should choose penalty flag always as N (No Penalty). Irrespective of whether the bank is

through front end or bulk upload. Sponsor bank should raise BGGD using remitting bank 
customer’s account number which helps issuing bank to remove the lien and debit the 
customer account. Once the adjustments are successfully settled, URCS will update the 
account number in adjustment report, refer SHDT74 column for details
