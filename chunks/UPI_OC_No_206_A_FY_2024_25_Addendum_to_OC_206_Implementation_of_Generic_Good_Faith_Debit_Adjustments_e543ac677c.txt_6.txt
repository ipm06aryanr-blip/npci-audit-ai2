ADJUSTMENTS LIFE CYCLE: Adjustment life cycle for the above generic good faith debit adjustments 
are as follows,  
i) 
Raise: BGGD can be raised by the IPO sponsor banks with mandate creation details  
ii) 
Acceptance: Issuing Bank who has received the generic good faith adjustments has to 
accept in URCS. 
iii) 
Re-presentment: Issuing Bank who has received the generic good faith adjustments may 
reject the adjustments (e.g. Duplicate execution, Initiation not done – Invalid execution 
etc.). 
iv) 
Deemed Acceptance: If issuing Bank has not accepted or re-presentment the BGGD within 
TAT of 3 calendar days, then adjustment window will be closed on deemed acceptance 
basis i.e. on 4th day. 
RBI PENALTY CALCULATION: 
 
No penalty is applicable for BGGD, because it’s an offline IPO/Mandate with block functionality
