As per the Volume Cap Circular, the following points are applicable: 
i. 
Payment Service Provider (“PSP”) and each TPAP should ensure that the total volume 
of the transactions initiated through the TPAP shall not exceed 30% of the overall 
volume of transactions processed in UPI, during the preceding three (3) months (on a 
rolling basis); and 
ii. 
The aforesaid Volume Cap is effective from 01st January, 2021. Existing TPAPs, 
which are exceeding the Volume Cap as on 31st December 2020, will have a period 
of two (2) years from the effective date of this Volume Cap to comply with the 
provisions hereof.
