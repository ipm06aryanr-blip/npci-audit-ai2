6. Total Volume of UPI Transactions shall be calculated basis the transactions processed in 
UPI during the preceding three (3) months (on a rolling basis). The calculation will be done 
basis the three (3) months volume of TPAP UPI transactions and three (3) months total 
Volume of UPI transactions (Previous 3 months from the month of calculation).   
 
7. The identifier for a TPAP shall be the unique UPI handle of the TPAP. For the multi-bank 
TPAP, cumulative volume generated from all the handles shall be considered.  
 
8. NPCI may also deal with outliers coming due to minor fluctuations and also by computing 
the quarterly average if the need arises.
