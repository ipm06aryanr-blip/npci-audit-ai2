During the Exemption, if permitted, the TPAP must adopt the new customer on-boarding in a 
moderated manner only. If it is observed that the TPAP continues to on-board new customers 
without any moderation, NPCI shall issue a notice to stop. The provision of Exemption will ensure 
that existing users are not put to inconvenience and new customer on-boarding is also not fully 
shut as mentioned in this SOP. This provision may help TPAP to continue to maintain the trust, 
while working towards to achieve the compliance.
