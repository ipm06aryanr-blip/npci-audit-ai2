smoothen out all the transactions in the UPI ecosystem. One such effort was UPI multi-bank 
model, which was implemented to mitigate the risks on the banks by means of mandating large 
TPAPs for the UPI access. The suggested measures in this SOP, shall also encourage the new 
players to increase their volumes in UPI, so as to even out the distribution of market share among 
all participants. While, recently UPI has admitted some of the new players, there have been
