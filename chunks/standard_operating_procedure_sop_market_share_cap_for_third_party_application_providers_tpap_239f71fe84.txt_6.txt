For the purpose of clarification, a ‘Payer TPAP’ shall mean a participant in the UPI which can 
onboard a customer, create his UPI ID, create device binding and UPI PIN; whereas, ‘Payee 
TPAP’ shall mean a participant in UPI who can on- board a customer or a merchant and allow 
them to receive money basis allocated UPI ID or raise a collect request. It is further clarified that 
there are no restrictions at a Payee TPAP’s end as far as Volume Cap is concerned and as Payee, 
TPAP may continue to onboard new customers and merchants for the purpose of receiving UPI 
payments.
