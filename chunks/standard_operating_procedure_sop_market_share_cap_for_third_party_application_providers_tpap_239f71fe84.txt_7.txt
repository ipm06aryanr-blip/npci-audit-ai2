1. ‘Total Volume of UPI Transactions’ shall mean the aggregate of all successful financial 
transactions in UPI ecosystem. Payments to UPI Id, account number + IFSC combination, 
the financial leg of the mandate transactions (both one-time mandate and recurring), 
payment to PPIs and other permissible account types which are being implemented in 
near future shall also be considered. This shall include all P2P, P2PM and P2M 
transactions performed in UPI through various modes like Push, Collect, Intent, QR or any 
other future modes including B2B and B2C transactions.  
 
2. ‘TPAP UPI Transactions’ shall mean the aggregate of all successful financial 
transactions on TPAP UPI App shall be considered for the computation. This shall include 
all P2P, P2PM and P2M transactions performed on TPAP UPI App through various modes 
like Push, Collect, Intent, QR or any other future modes. Payments to UPI Id, account
